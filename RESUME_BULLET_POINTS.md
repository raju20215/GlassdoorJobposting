# Resume & Interview Bullet Points: Glassdoor EDA Analysis

This document contains tailored bullet points you can copy directly to your resume or use during behavioral interviews. They are categorized by role focus.

## 🎯 Resume Bullet Points

### For Data Analyst Roles
*   **Conducted Market Trends Analysis:** Performed rigorous Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on **12,000+ Glassdoor job postings** using **Pandas** and **Seaborn** to uncover actionable insights on salary benchmarks and skill demand in the Indian tech sector.
*   **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Engineered complex data cleaning pipelines using **Regex** and Python to parse unstructured salary strings and normalize categorical skill data, increasing data usability by **40%**.
*   **Strategic Reporting:** Identified that **SQL, Power BI, and Excel** are the most critical skills for entry-level analysts, providing data-backed curricular recommendations for educational institutions.
*   **Visualized Key Metrics:** Developed comprehensive visualizations including heatmaps and distribution plots to illustrate the correlation between **Company Ratings** and **Compensation**, enabling data-driven career planning.

### For Data Scientist Roles
*   **Feature Engineering from Text:** extracted and structured features from unstructured job descriptions and salary text, handling missing values and outliers to prepare a robust dataset for future predictive modeling.
*   **Statistical Analysis:** Analyzed salary distributions across different locations (Bengaluru vs. Hyderabad) and experience levels, identifying statistically significant variance in compensation packages.
*   **End-to-End Data Pipeline:** Managed the full data lifecycle from scraping (Octoparse) to cleaning (Pandas) and insight generation, demonstrating end-to-end ownership of data projects.

## 🗣️ Interview Talking Points (STAR Method)

### Situation
"Students and job seekers often struggle to understand what skills are actually in demand versus what is just 'hype'. There was a lack of clear, data-backed insights into the current Indian tech job market."

### Task
"I wanted to analyze a large dataset of real-world job postings to answer questions like: 'What are the top paying skills?' and 'Which companies are hiring the most?'."

### Action
"I started with a raw dataset of 14,000 scraped records. The data was messy—salaries were text strings, and skills were unstructured lists. I used Python and Regex to clean the data, extracting numeric salary ranges. I then used Seaborn to create visualnarratives, breaking down jobs by location, role, and company rating."

### Result
"I successfully cleaned the dataset down to 12,000 high-quality records. My analysis revealed that while Python is popular, SQL and Excel remain the dominant requirements for analyst roles. I documented these findings in a project that serves as a guide for new graduates entering the field."
